FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I start a project?

Submit the project request form with your requirements. We'll review it and contact you to discuss the scope, timeline and pricing.

Do you work on Android projects?

Yes. Android development can include Kotlin, Firebase, APIs, databases, UI implementation and project guidance.

Can I request only a PPT or report?

Yes. Individual services such as PPT design, report formatting and diagrams are available.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing depends on complexity, scope, deadline and the services requested. You'll receive a quote before work begins.

Can you explain the project after delivery?

Yes. Project walkthroughs, documentation explanations and viva preparation can be requested.

Can I order multiple services?

Yes. You can combine development, UI design, documentation, PPT design and demo preparation into one project package.
